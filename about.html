Starlight Hospice is based in Burbank and serves all of Los Angeles County and the surrounding areas.

Our approach

We provide patient centered care that protects and improves quality of life on every level: physical, emotional, interpersonal, and spiritual.

Hospice care supports a person’s comfort, wellbeing, relationships, and faith. It also supports the family and caregivers, before and after a loss.

As a family owned hospice, we know how much trust it takes to invite a care team into your life at such an important time. We work to earn that trust with every visit.

What guides us

Four things we bring to every home.

  • Comfort

    Expert pain and symptom management, so patients can rest and spend time with the people they love.

  • Dignity

    Care that honors each patient’s wishes, culture, faith, and way of life.

  • Family

    Guidance and support for caregivers, including grief support after a loss.

  • Availability

    Phones answered 24/7 and after hours nurses who can advise by phone or visit when needed.
