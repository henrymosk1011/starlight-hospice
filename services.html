Services

Find the right care.Find the right care.

Care needs change over time, and hospice adapts to those needs. We offer a wide variety of care from one coordinated team.

  • Physicians on staff
  • Nurses on staff
  • Hospice aides
  • Palliative care
  • Social workers
  • Medical team
  • Specialized programs
  • Bereavement support
Expert pain and symptom management

Physicians on staff

Our physicians are board certified in hospice and palliative medicine. They specialize in managing pain and symptoms caused by life limiting illness and at the end of life.

They are available around the clock for consultation and to address symptoms as they arise.

  • Board certified in hospice and palliative medicine
  • Available 24 hours a day for consultation
  • Work alongside your own physician
Coordinated, hands on care

Nurses on staff

Our team includes registered nurses (RNs) and licensed vocational nurses (LVNs) who serve in many roles, from care coordination and case management to direct patient care and leadership.

Our after hours nurses can give advice by phone or make a home visit when needed.

  • RNs and LVNs
  • Care coordination and case management
  • After hours phone advice and home visits
Daily comfort and personal care

Hospice aides

Our Certified Home Health Aides (CHHAs) help families with patient care, including bathing and basic homemaker services.

They guide and support caregivers with activities of daily living such as dressing, feeding, transferring, and toileting.

  • Bathing and personal care
  • Light homemaker services
  • Hands on guidance for family caregivers
Relief earlier in an illness

Palliative care

Starlight Hospice provides palliative care for patients who need help relieving pain and symptoms at an earlier stage of a disease.

  • Symptom relief at any stage of illness
  • Focus on comfort and quality of life
Support for the whole family

Social workers

Our masters level medical social workers care for the emotional needs and overall wellbeing of patients and families, and help them navigate the many decisions they face at the end of life.

  • Help with difficult family conversations
  • Finding resources and advocating for you
  • Assistance with placement needs
One team around each patient

Medical team

Our medical team brings together doctors, nurses, therapists, and home health aides who work as one team around each patient and family.

  • Doctors and nurses
  • Therapists
  • Home health aides
For managed care clients

Specialized programs

We offer three specialized programs designed to meet the unique needs of our managed care clients.

  • Mi Amado
  • ESRD (end stage renal disease)
  • Transitional Palliative Care
Care that continues after a loss

Bereavement support

Hospice care is for the patient and the people who love them. That care does not stop after a patient dies.

Starlight Hospice provides grief support to families for at least a year after a loss.

  • Grief support for at least a year
  • Check ins and resources for loved ones
