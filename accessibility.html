Accessibility statement.Accessibility statement.

Starlight Hospice is committed to making this website usable for everyone, including people with disabilities.

Our standard

WCAG 2.2, Level AA.

This website is designed to conform to the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2 at Level AA.

These guidelines explain how to make web content more accessible for people who are blind or have low vision, are deaf or hard of hearing, or have motor, speech, cognitive, or learning disabilities.

Built in

Features of this site

  • A “Skip to main content” link at the top of every page
  • Full keyboard navigation with a clearly visible focus outline
  • Headings, landmarks, and labels that work with screen readers
  • Text and controls that meet WCAG color contrast requirements
  • Text that can be enlarged to 200% without loss of content
  • Animations that follow your device’s reduced motion setting, plus a “Reduce motion” switch in the menu and footer of every page; no animation plays for more than five seconds on its own
  • Focused links and buttons are never hidden behind other parts of the page
  • Layouts that adapt to enlarged text, increased text spacing, and screens as narrow as 320 pixels
  • Form fields with visible labels and clear error messages
